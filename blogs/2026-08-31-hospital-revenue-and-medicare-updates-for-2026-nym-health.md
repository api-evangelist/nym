In 2026, the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) cut work relative value units (RVUs) by 2.5% on nearly 7,000 physician service codes, a change that touches about 91% of the services physicians bill to Medicare (1). And that's just one of many reimbursement changes that hospital finance and revenue cycle management (RCM) teams need on their radar as they look ahead to 2027.
