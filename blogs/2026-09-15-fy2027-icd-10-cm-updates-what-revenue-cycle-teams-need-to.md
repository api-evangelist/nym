On October 1, 2026, the FY2027 ICD-10-CM update takes effect for all discharges and encounters from that date forward. By the numbers, it is a quieter year: 190 new reportable diagnosis codes, 30 formerly reportable codes deleted, and four revised code titles, compared with 487 new codes in FY2026.
